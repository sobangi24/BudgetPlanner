##Budget Planner

A React + Vite budget planner app that lets you track your monthly income and expenses with a simple, spreadsheet-style interface. Each month has its own data, and the app calculates total income, expenses, and savings for easy budget management.

##Features 
Month Selector: Switch between months to track different budgets.
Income & Expenses Tables: Add, view, and delete transactions.
Balance Display: See total income, expenses, and savings per month.
Responsive Design: Clean, modern layout that works on desktop and mobile.
